**Founding badge in 3 steps**

1. ⭐ Star flisk (runs your microservices and agent stacks locally with one command) and follow flisk-sh: https://github.com/flisk-sh/flisk
2. ✅ Get a PR merged. Good first issues: https://github.com/flisk-sh/flisk/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22 Then post it on X: say what you fixed and link the PR.
3. 💬 Post the PR link in this channel. Within minutes the bot gives you your number, your role and the private #contributors channel with the maintainers.

First 100 only.
